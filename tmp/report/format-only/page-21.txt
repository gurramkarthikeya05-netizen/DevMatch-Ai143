The job module allows recruiters to create, edit and remove their own postings. Required 
skill values are stored as job_skills child records. The application module checks whether 
the job exists, whether its deadline has passed and whether the developer already applied. 
A successful submission creates an application and commits it to MySQL.
Skill matching and initial status
Let R be the set of case normalized job skill names and C the union of profile skill names 
and the latest extracted resume skills. The displayed score is round(100 × |R ∩ C| / |R|), or 
zero when R is empty. The shortlist decision uses the unrounded fraction and a strict 
threshold above 70 percent.

A candidate matching three of three skills scores 100 percent and meets the threshold; 
two of three scores 67 percent and does not. Exactly seven of ten skills produces 70 
percent and is rejected initially. A job with no recorded skills also does not meet the 
threshold. The recruiter can change the status to Applied, Under Review, Shortlisted, 
Rejected or Selected after review.
The function exposes matched, missing, profile matched and resume matched skill lists. 
These lists explain the score. Matching is based on recorded names, not semantic 
equivalence, and does not use proficiency levels, years, minimum experience or protected 
personal characteristics.
