DESIGN
The design separates shared account information from developer and recruiter data. Jobs 
belong to recruiters, while applications connect developers to jobs. Repeated skill and 
technology values are represented by child rows. Figure 3.1 summarizes the key links in 
the physical schema.
3.1 Schema Diagram
Fig 3.1 Relational schema
PK denotes a primary key, FK a foreign key and UK a unique key. Ten tables are created by the 
application models. The eleventh table, application_status_history, is an audit extension installed 
by mysql_demo.sql. The unique developer and job pair in applications prevents duplicate 
applications.
